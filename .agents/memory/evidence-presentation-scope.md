name: Evidence presentation scope
description: Approved boundary between neutral evidence labels and stored editorial findings.
---

Treat historical ad captures as evidence of past observations, not proof of current campaign activity. A weekly creative count alone does not establish a rising, stable, or declining trend. Featured homepage cards use neutral investigation framing even when scores are high.

**Why:** The user explicitly approved bounded presentation corrections after historical captures and low-score investigations were given stronger activity/scam labels by templates.

**How to apply:** Keep initial HTML and interactive wording consistent, retain numerical scores, evidence identifiers, and safe source links. Report contradictory stored editorial prose separately rather than silently rewriting it or changing database records. Broader editorial corrections require separate approval.
